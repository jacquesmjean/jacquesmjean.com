## Saturday, November 28, 8:30 AM Central. "What If Intelligence Is the New Currency?"

Image: let LinkedIn pull the card (the doctor at the kitchen table). Link in the post.

A close friend called me this month. He is a physician who built a health app. Smart man, real users, his own money.

His AI bill runs past a thousand dollars some months. He pays a second company overseas to keep the thing alive. And one morning his app was gone from Android. Nobody asked him.

"I have no control over any of it," he said. "I think I have to let it go."

On Thanksgiving night I rewatched "In Time," the film where time is money and the poor die at the end of the day. I could not sleep afterward, because I had just watched my friend live it.

The price of a token fell nearly 300 times in two years. His bill went up. The minute got cheaper and the meter ran faster.

Four companies will spend about 700 billion dollars this year building the clock. Africa, with 1.4 billion people and the minerals inside every server, holds 0.6 percent of the world's data center capacity.

The villain in the film is a man in a suit. In life there is no man. The meter does not hate you. It does not need to.

I wrote about who owns the clock, who sets the rules, and the one move left to the rest of us.

https://jacquesmjean.com/perspectives/what-if-intelligence-is-the-new-currency-in-time/

My friend is starting over. This time he will own it. The rest of us still have a choice.

## Monday, December 1, 7:30 AM Central. "How Three Signed Deals Slipped Away"

Image: the Christmas photo (link card) or the embassy photo with Ambassador FitzPatrick and Aymeric. Tag Aymeric Saha and the American Business Council Gabon if you are comfortable.

In September 2024 I signed three agreements with the government of Gabon at the U.S. Chamber of Commerce. The president was in the room. So was our ambassador.

The first thing a Gabonese delegate told me afterward was not congratulations. It was a warning. "American companies sign in Washington and stay in Washington. The Chinese show up."

So I showed up. December in Libreville, as promised. Every ministry, every program lead, every document. Both projects cleared procurement in January.

Then one word stopped everything: financing. We did not have a bank. So we went to the agencies that exist for exactly this. "We only do infrastructure." "Not a priority at this time." "Reviewing our pipeline." Meeting, follow-up, silence.

Months later I called to set up a meeting and was told my contact could not make it. He was in China.

By this month all three agreements belonged to Huawei.

Nobody in Gabon did anything wrong. They waited longer for us than they had to. I wrote down what happened, what I would do differently, and the one thing Washington should build.

https://jacquesmjean.com/perspectives/how-three-signed-deals-slipped-away-gabon-huawei/

Next time, I will bring the bank.
