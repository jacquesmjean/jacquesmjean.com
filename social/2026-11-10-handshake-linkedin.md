## Post (main)

In September 2024, I signed three agreements with the government of Gabon at the U.S. Chamber of Commerce.

The president was in the room. So was the U.S. ambassador. So were my CFO, my VP of International Business Development, and my Head of Marketing. Four of us flew to Washington because the opportunity was real.

This week I watched the last of those three deals go to Huawei.

Not because Huawei built something better. Because China kept showing up and we did not.

Last Thursday Beijing gave Gabon's president an honor guard, a state banquet, and six signed agreements. In July 2025, Washington gave him a crowded lunch with four other heads of state. Our embassy in Libreville has had no confirmed ambassador for most of this year.

I spent 25 years building technology systems in 15 countries. I learned one thing early: the company that installs the system usually runs it for a generation. Now apply that to a country.

African leaders are not choosing China over America. They are choosing the partner who shows up. The door in Libreville is still open. Every empty chair pushes it a little closer to shut.

I wrote down what I saw, and what has to change. Link below. The French edition is there too for my friends in Libreville, Dakar, and Paris.

https://jacquesmjean.com/perspectives/handshake-is-the-deal-us-china-africa-gabon/

If you do business in Africa, I would like to hear whether you are seeing the same thing.
